Motion

Arrive
Spring, stiffness 380, damping 30. One arc, no bounce on the list.
Press
Scale to 0.94 in 160 ms, release on the same curve.
Fuse
Burns down over 24 h. Never animated on screen.
Reduced motion
No flight. Items appear in place.

Rules

  • Amber means "this sends something". Nothing else is amber.
  • One loud element per screen.
  • Previews are always the real file.
  • Edges are 1px hairlines. Only things that float (popover, dragged file) get a soft shadow.
  • One typeface, three weights: 400, 500, 600.
  • Touch targets 44 pt on phone, hit areas extended on Mac.
