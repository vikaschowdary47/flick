PROBLEM AND WHY NOW

Phone-to-computer is still the slowest hop in your day.

Moving one file takes too many steps

Email it to yourself, AirDrop only between Apple devices, cables, cloud drives you then have to dig through. Getting a photo or PDF from your phone to your laptop is still slow.

Teams hand off files through the wrong tools

Work files end up in personal apps, email threads and chat messages. They get lost, live on forever, and nobody controls where they went.

Why now

Most people work across a phone and a laptop that run different systems, and their files are mostly made on the phone. A lightweight, cross-platform hand-off fills the gap.
